01 / The program

Make Korea
your classroom.

Connect what you study with what you experience.

Two APR courses taught by Alabama faculty at Yonsei University in Seoul. Class sessions come first, over the opening days of the trip; the rest of the three weeks takes you into the newsrooms, studios and companies you just finished reading about. Weekends are free.

Applications are open · Deadline October 30

Apply through the university's Faculty-Led Program General Application and select UA in South Korea as your top choice. Every applicant then meets with Dr. Ki for a short interview during the first two weeks of November.

You do not have to be ready to apply to get in touch. If you are still weighing it — cost, housing, whether your major fits — email Dr. Ki and ask.

03 / Academics

Two courses.
A global context.

Students take both courses for a total of six credits.

Graduate students may enroll at the 590 level.

APR 490 / 5903 credit hours

Advertising in Korea:
From Street to Screens

How Korean advertising moves from subway posters and neighborhood signage to the screens that carry Korean products worldwide.

  • Agencies, stakeholders and decision-making
  • Cross-cultural social media analysis
  • Integrated marketing communication
Dr. Hyoungkoo Khang
APR 490 / 5903 credit hours

Public Relations in Korea:
From Culture to Global Impact

How Korean organizations build reputation at home and turn cultural strength — music, film, food, tech — into global influence.

  • Public relations roles, tactics and strategies
  • Communication across sectors and cultures
  • Opportunities and challenges in Korean markets
Dr. Eyun-Jung Ki

All majors welcome.  No prerequisites. Recent groups have included students from nursing, business and the honors college.

Before you go

How do I apply?

Complete the Faculty-Led Program General Application and select UA in South Korea as your top choice. The deadline is October 30, and an interview is required — interviews are scheduled during the first two weeks of November. Email Dr. Ki at ki@apr.ua.edu with any question before you submit, or simply to learn more.

Faculty-Led Program General Application ↗

Who can participate?

Students from all majors are welcome. The courses have no prerequisites, but registration requires instructor approval and completion of study abroad requirements.

What does the program cost?

The next program's fee has not been announced. Contact Dr. Ki for the current budget, payment schedule, and included expenses.

What has the program included?

Previous program information included six credits of tuition, accommodations, ground transportation, excursions, entrance fees, insurance, some meals, and round-trip Jeju flights. International airfare, some meals, and personal expenses were excluded. Inclusions for the next program will be confirmed with its budget.

Where do students stay and study?

Previous arrangements included hotels rated three stars or above with two students per room, and classroom space at Yonsei University within walking distance of the hotel. Arrangements may change for the next program.

Can I use scholarships or financial aid?

Ask your financial aid advisor about your eligibility and available funding. Explore study abroad scholarships, including C&IS opportunities.
