The program

Class in the morning. The industry in the afternoon.

You take two APR courses taught by Alabama faculty at a partner university in Seoul — then walk out of the classroom and into the places you just studied. Broadcast studios. A search giant's headquarters. A live music show taping. The people who build Korean brands and export Korean culture.

Weekends belong to the rest of the country: a palace in a hanbok, a baseball game with a fight song section, and the coastline of Jeju Island.

Courses · 6 credit hours

Two courses, both taught in Korea.

APR 490 / 590 · 3 credits

Advertising in Korea: From Street to Screens

Dr. Hyoungkoo Khang

How Korean advertising moves from subway posters and neighborhood signage to the screens that carry Korean products worldwide.

APR 490 / 590 · 3 credits

Public Relations in Korea: From Culture to Global Impact

Dr. Eyun-Jung Ki

How Korean organizations build reputation at home and turn cultural strength — music, film, food, tech — into global influence.

All majors welcome. No prerequisites. Students take both courses for a full six credit hours, and graduate students may enroll at the 590 level.

Site visits

The syllabus has an address.

Every unit comes with a visit. Past groups have gone inside the newsrooms, studios, and platforms below — and sat down with the professionals who run them.

Where you go

Seoul and Jeju every year. The rest changes.

Two anchors stay fixed on every itinerary. Beyond them, each cohort gets a different corner of the country — so no two years of this program see the same Korea.

Every year

Seoul

Home base for three weeks — classes, company visits, palaces, night markets, and a KBO baseball game.

Every year

Jeju Island

Volcanic coastline, black-sand beaches, and the calmest days of the trip.

New in 2027

Gyeonggi & Gangwon Provinces

Next summer's group heads into the provinces just outside Seoul and across to the east coast. Recent cohorts went to Gyeongju and Busan instead.
